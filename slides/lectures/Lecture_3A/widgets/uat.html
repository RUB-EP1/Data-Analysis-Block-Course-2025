Universal approximation, one unit at a time

— target   — network g(x)

One hidden layer: g(x) = c0 + Σj vj h(wj x + bj). Here the weights are constructed, not trained: each unit adds one step (sigmoid) or one kink (ReLU).

Increase the number of units and watch the error fall. The theorem says such weights exist; it says nothing about how many units you need or whether training finds them.
