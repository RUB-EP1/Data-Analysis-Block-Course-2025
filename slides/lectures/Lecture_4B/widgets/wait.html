“Wait”: forcing the model to think longer

a constructed example to show the mechanism

When the model tries to end its thinking, the harness can refuse and append the word “Wait” instead. The model continues, and often checks or corrects itself (budget forcing, s1, 2025).

Real reasoning models learn when to write “wait” during reinforcement learning (DeepSeek-R1, 2025). More thinking helps up to a point; beyond it, extra tokens only cost time and money.
