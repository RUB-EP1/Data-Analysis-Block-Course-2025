Boosting a classifier with binary cross-entropy

● signal y = 1   ● background y = 0

Trees add to the log-odds F(x); p = σ(F). Point area ∝ |y − p|, the size of the pseudo-residual. Leaf penalty λ = 1; 300 training, 1000 validation events; up to 200 trees.
